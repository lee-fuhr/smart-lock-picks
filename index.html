How we choose our picks

Our recommendations start with reputation and long-term owner feedback gathered from forums, retailer reviews, and years of community discussion. We cross-check that against published expert reviews and teardown analyses to see how models behave after the honeymoon period, not just on day one. We also pay close attention to what actually breaks: stripped mounting screws, finicky keypads, battery drain, and Wi-Fi dropouts matter far more than a flashy spec sheet. We don’t rank locks by marketing claims or first-week impressions. Instead, we track real-world reports over time and update our picks when firmware updates or hardware revisions change the picture. If a product has a known weakness, we call it out, because the right smart lock for you depends on your door, your phone, and, if you rent, your landlord’s rules.
